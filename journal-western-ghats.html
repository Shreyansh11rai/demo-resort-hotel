48 hours in the Western Ghats.

A little itinerary for doing less, noticing more and finding your own rhythm in the hills.

There's no need to make the most of every minute. The point of a short escape is to let a few of them pass by without a plan.

Day one: arrive, exhale

Take the scenic drive in, then give yourself permission to do very little. Settle in with a cup of something warm. Wander the grounds if the mood takes you, or simply watch the afternoon light shift across the hills.

At dinner, try what's in season. Ask the kitchen what came in fresh that morning; the answer is usually a good place to start.

Day two: follow the morning

If you wake early, take a gentle walk while the air is still cool. If not, a slow breakfast will be waiting. The day might bring a trail with a local guide, a quiet hour by the pool or a spa treatment that stretches into the afternoon.

Leave room between plans. Some of the best moments have a way of showing up in the gaps.

Day three: take the feeling with you

One last breakfast, one last look at the hills, then a leisurely journey home. The best souvenir might be the reminder that a little more space is possible.
