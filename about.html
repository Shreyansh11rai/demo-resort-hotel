A gentler pace

Rooted in the land. Open to the world.

Set among the forested slopes of Maharashtra's Western Ghats, The Retreat was imagined as a natural extension of its landscape. Rather than change the view, we wanted to create a place from which to truly see it.

Our small collection of rooms and hideaways celebrates the textures around us: locally made craft, honest materials and spaces shaped by light. Nothing asks for your attention. Everything invites you to stay a little longer.
