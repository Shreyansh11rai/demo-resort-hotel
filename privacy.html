Privacy notice.

How information is handled on this demonstration website.

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This is sample policy content for a demonstration website. The Retreat is a fictional hospitality brand in this demo. This site does not operate a booking engine, process payments, or send enquiry data to a server.

Information you enter

Contact and enquiry forms on this demo site are front-end demonstrations only. Submitting a form displays an on-screen message; information is not transmitted, saved or shared by the website.

Cookies and analytics

This demonstration does not intentionally use analytics, advertising cookies or user accounts. Your browser or third-party services used to load fonts and placeholder photographs may process technical information under their own terms.

External services

Some pages load sample imagery from Unsplash and fonts from Google Fonts. When those resources load, your browser connects directly to the respective service. Consult their policies for details.

Your choices

You may choose not to enter information in the demo form. For a live property website, a complete privacy notice should identify the responsible legal entity, explain lawful bases and retention periods, and provide the correct contact details for privacy requests.

Contact

For this fictional demonstration, contact privacy@theretreat.example. Replace this address and all sample policy text before launching any real service.
