Eating with the seasons.

Meet the local growers and thoughtful ingredients behind a meal at The Retreat.

Our favourite menus are the ones that begin with a question: what's good right now? The answer changes with the weather, the market and the people who tend the land around us.

Closer to the source

Whenever we can, our kitchen works with nearby growers and makers. Knowing where an ingredient comes from changes how you cook with it — and how you enjoy it. A just-picked vegetable needs little more than care, good heat and a place at the table.

Let the season lead

Some days call for a bowl of something warm and familiar. Others bring bright herbs, crisp fruit or a new idea from the garden. Our menus follow those small shifts rather than a fixed calendar.

Pull up a chair

Ask what's on the menu today. Share an extra plate. Make lunch last a little longer than planned. That's our favourite kind of dining.
