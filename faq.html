Before you arrive

Frequently asked.

Where is The Retreat located?

We're a fictional demonstration retreat set near Malshej Ghat in Maharashtra's Western Ghats. See our location page for illustrative travel information.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Demo timings are check-in from 2:00 pm and check-out by 11:00 am. Early arrivals or late departures may be requested in advance and are subject to availability.

Is breakfast included?

Yes, daily breakfast for registered guests is included in the illustrative room rates shown on this demo website.

Are children welcome?

Absolutely. Families are welcome, and the The Retreat Villa is well suited to travelling together. Please contact our team for age-specific arrangements.

Can you accommodate dietary needs?

Our kitchen can accommodate most dietary preferences with advance notice. Please let us know when making an enquiry so our team can confirm what's possible.

Are pets allowed?

Pet arrangements are assessed case by case. Please contact us before booking to discuss your needs.

Is the resort accessible?

Some accessible routes and accommodation features are available, but the natural hillside setting may present limitations. Contact us to discuss specific requirements before booking.

Can I arrange a transfer?

Private transfers may be arranged in advance, subject to availability. Share your travel details with our reservations team.

How do I cancel or change a booking?

Demo bookings are not live. For a real property, refer to the applicable booking confirmation and cancellation policy.

Are these rates and offers real?

No. This is a demonstration website. All pricing, availability, facilities, timings and contact information are sample content only.
