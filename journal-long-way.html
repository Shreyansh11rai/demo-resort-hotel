Take the long way round.

Five small reasons to leave the map in your pocket and follow the path instead.

A walk doesn't have to take you anywhere in particular. Around here, that's often the best part.

Notice the little things

The forest has its own quiet detail: a bird call, a leaf newly turned, the smell of rain arriving over the ridge. Walk slowly enough and the world starts to offer them up.

Let the conversation wander

Side-by-side, without the usual distractions, people tend to talk differently. A simple walk can make room for the kind of conversation that never quite fits into a busy day.

Find a view you didn't expect

Take a turn you haven't taken before. A small rise, a bend in the path, an unplanned pause — the view might be familiar, but the way you meet it won't be.

Come back a little lighter

No step count required. Just a little time outside, at your own pace. When you're ready, the table and a cup of tea are waiting.
