Accessibility.

Information about using this demonstration website and planning a visit.

Our intention

We want people to be able to explore The Retreat in the way that works for them. This demo uses semantic page landmarks, labelled enquiry fields, descriptive image text and responsive layouts. It has not been independently audited and may not meet every accessibility requirement.

Using the website

Most pages can be navigated with a keyboard. Gallery photos can be opened by activating them and closed with the on-screen close control or by clicking outside the image. The mobile navigation has a labelled toggle.

Visiting the property

The resort and its accessibility features described here are fictional. The natural hillside setting may include uneven paths and steps. Contact a real property directly to discuss room access, bathrooms, routes, transport, dietary needs and any other requirements before booking.

Feedback

If you encounter a barrier on this demo site, please use the contact page to let us know. Its form is only illustrative and does not send messages.
